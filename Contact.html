Frequently Asked Question

What payment methods does QuickKart accept?

QuickKart offers a variety of secure and convenient payment methods to provide a seamless shopping experience. Customers can pay using credit and debit cards such as Visa and Mastercard for quick transactions. The platform also supports UPI (Unified Payments Interface), enabling instant payments through apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm. Users can choose net banking to directly transfer money from their bank accounts. Additionally, QuickKart accepts digital wallets for faster checkout. For users who prefer offline options, Cash on Delivery (COD) is available. In some cases, Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) and EMI options are also provided for flexible payments.

How long does delivery take?

QuickKart usually delivers orders within 3 to 7 business days across India after the product is successfully processed and shipped. The exact delivery time may vary depending on the customer’s location, availability of the product, and courier service speed. In metro cities, delivery is often faster, while remote or rural areas may take slightly longer. After placing an order, customers receive a tracking link so they can monitor their shipment in real time. QuickKart aims to ensure fast, safe, and reliable delivery so that customers receive their products in good condition and within the estimated timeframe.

Can I return or exchange a product?

Yes, QuickKart allows customers to return or exchange products if they are not satisfied with their purchase. Returns or exchanges are accepted within a specified time period, usually 7 to 10 days after delivery, depending on the product category. The product must be in its original condition, unused, and with all packaging and tags intact. Once the return request is approved, a pickup is arranged from the customer’s address. After the product is received and verified, a refund or replacement is processed as per the customer’s choice. QuickKart aims to ensure a hassle-free and customer-friendly return and exchange policy.

How can I track my order?

How can I track my order? Customers can easily track their orders on QuickKart using the order tracking feature. After placing an order, a confirmation message and tracking ID are sent to the registered email or mobile number. By logging into the QuickKart account and visiting the “My Orders” section, users can view real-time updates of their shipment status. Alternatively, the tracking ID can be entered on the courier partner’s tracking page to check delivery progress. Updates include order confirmation, dispatch, in-transit status, and expected delivery date. This helps customers stay informed about their order until it is safely delivered to their address.
